## 🌐 About TechTide

Technology evolves every day. New AI models are released, developer tools improve, frameworks change, startups launch, and major companies announce products that can reshape the industry.

Keeping up with these developments should not require spending hours browsing different platforms.

TechTide is being developed to make technology discovery simpler, more organized, and more useful.

The platform aims to provide a centralized technology feed with relevant updates, concise summaries, topic-based personalization, original source links, and contextual insights that help readers understand the impact of important developments.

Our goal is not simply to deliver more information. It is to help users discover the information that deserves their attention.

## 🚨 The Problem

Technology information is distributed across multiple platforms, including company blogs, research publications, developer communities, news websites, and social media.

This creates several challenges:

- **Information fragmentation:** Users must visit several sources to understand what's happening across the technology industry.
- **Information overload:** Important announcements compete with repetitive stories, minor updates, and less relevant content.
- **Time constraints:** Students and developers often have limited time to keep up with industry developments.
- **Lack of personalization:** A developer interested in Java, backend engineering, and cloud computing may not want the same feed as someone focused on AI research.
- **Limited context:** A headline alone rarely explains why a new model, framework, or product matters.
- **Difficulty discovering opportunities:** Useful repositories, documentation, tutorials, and practical learning resources can be difficult to find among general news.

TechTide aims to address these problems through a focused, personalized technology discovery experience.

## 🎯 Our Vision

To build a reliable technology intelligence platform that helps people stay informed, understand emerging trends, and make better decisions about what they learn and build.

TechTide aims to evolve beyond traditional news aggregation by connecting industry developments with practical knowledge.

Our long-term vision includes:

- A personalized technology intelligence feed.
- Concise, source-backed AI-generated summaries.
- Coverage of important technology announcements and releases.
- Connections between news, official documentation, and developer resources.
- Trending technology topics and emerging industry patterns.
- A daily briefing that helps users stay informed in less time.

## ✨ Key Features

The features below describe the product direction. Individual features will become available as they are implemented and tested.

### 📰 1. Unified Technology News Feed

Discover technology developments in one place.

Planned coverage includes:

- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
- Large Language Models and AI agents.
- Software engineering and programming.
- Web and backend development.
- Cloud computing and DevOps.
- Cybersecurity and infrastructure.
- Open-source projects and developer tools.
- Startups, funding, and technology businesses.
- Emerging technologies and research.

### 🤖 2. AI-Powered News Summaries

Make complex technology developments easier to understand.

The planned summarization experience aims to explain:

- **What happened?** A concise account of the announcement.
- **Why does it matter?** The potential impact on developers, businesses, or users.
- **What's next?** Relevant resources, implications, or areas worth exploring.

Summaries should preserve important context, distinguish confirmed facts from speculation, and link back to original sources.

### 🎯 3. Personalized News Feed

Users should be able to select the topics most relevant to their interests.

For example:

- A Java developer could prioritize backend engineering, JVM updates, databases, and cloud technologies.
- An AI enthusiast could follow model releases, research, AI infrastructure, and developer APIs.
- A student preparing for software engineering roles could prioritize industry developments, open-source projects, and engineering tools.

The objective is to make each user's feed more relevant without overwhelming them with unnecessary updates.

### 🔥 4. Trending Technology

Discover developments receiving significant attention across selected technology sources.

Potential features include:

- Trending topics and announcements.
- Frequently discussed open-source projects.
- Major product launches.
- Important model and framework releases.
- Emerging industry themes.

Trending rankings should be based on transparent signals, such as source frequency, recency, and engagement where reliable data is available.

### 🔗 5. Original Sources and Further Reading

Trustworthy information matters more than a catchy headline.

TechTide aims to provide links to original announcements, official documentation, research papers, and relevant developer resources wherever possible.

Readers should be able to verify claims and explore the underlying material themselves.

### 💻 6. Developer Resources

Connect technology news with practical learning opportunities.

Potential integrations include:

- Relevant GitHub repositories.
- Official documentation.
- Tutorials and technical guides.
- Research papers.
- Product documentation and release notes.
- Project ideas inspired by new technologies.

### 📬 7. Daily Technology Briefing

A personalized briefing could summarize the most relevant developments from a selected period.

Potential delivery options include a website dashboard and an email newsletter.

The goal is to help users understand the most important developments without needing to browse every source manually.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Follow these steps once the application code and required configuration are available.

### Prerequisites

Install the following tools:

- Node.js and npm.
- Git.
- A code editor such as Visual Studio Code.
- PostgreSQL or the database service selected for the project, if required.

### 1. Clone the Repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/techtide.git
cd techtide
```

Replace `YOUR_USERNAME` with your GitHub username and update the repository URL if necessary.

### 2. Install Dependencies

```bash
npm install
```

### 3. Configure Environment Variables

Create a local `.env.local` file if the application uses environment variables.

Example configuration:

```env
DATABASE_URL="your_database_connection_string"
AI_API_KEY="your_ai_provider_api_key"
NEWS_API_KEY="your_news_provider_api_key"
```

These are illustrative placeholders, not confirmed requirements. Use only the variables required by your implementation.

**Security note:** Never commit real API keys, database passwords, or other secrets to GitHub. Keep local environment files out of version control and provide an `.env.example` containing placeholder values.

### 4. Start the Development Server

For a Next.js application:

```bash
npm run dev
```

Open the local development URL shown in your terminal, typically `http://localhost:3000`.

### 5. Build for Production

```bash
npm run build
npm run start
```

These commands assume the corresponding scripts are configured in `package.json`.

For the actual implementation, follow the repository's installed dependencies, environment configuration, and setup instructions.

## 📰 Data Sources and Content

TechTide aims to provide useful, attributable technology information from reliable sources.

Potential source categories include:

- Official company engineering blogs.
- Product and developer release notes.
- Research publications.
- Open-source project announcements.
- Technology news publishers.
- Permitted RSS feeds and APIs.

Source selection should consider reliability, relevance, freshness, and coverage.

### Content Principles

- Link to the original source whenever possible.
- Clearly identify the publisher and publication date.
- Avoid presenting unverified claims as facts.
- Preserve relevant context when summarizing announcements.
- Avoid duplicate stories that add little value.
- Respect publishers' terms, copyright, API restrictions, and robots policies where applicable.
- Do not republish full articles without appropriate permission.

TechTide aims to help readers discover and understand original reporting rather than replace it.

## 🛡️ Responsible Use of AI

AI-generated summaries can omit context, misinterpret technical details, or introduce factual errors.

TechTide aims to use AI as an aid to understanding, not as a replacement for reliable sources.

The intended approach includes:

- Preserving links to original sources.
- Evaluating summary quality.
- Avoiding unsupported claims.
- Distinguishing announcements from speculation.
- Reviewing important technical claims where appropriate.
- Providing transparency about AI-generated content.

The objective is to make technology information easier to understand without sacrificing trust.
